01 / The work

Make something worth returning to.

XyLyX Studios is a home for original creations: songs, visual pieces and experiments that bring the two together. Existing published work gives this exploration a concrete starting point.

Music & releases ↗ · Visual work ↗

No claim is made that the catalogue has generated repeat demand or sustainable revenue.

02 / The hypothesis

First attention isn't repeat demand.

Test whether people return for a second work or later release; actively opt in for updates; give specific feedback; and express meaningful collaboration interest. Distinguish casual viewers, close contacts and genuine independent listeners.

The audience, channel and measurement method must be defined before treating any observed numbers as evidence.

03 / The lean experiment

Learn before expanding.

  1. Use the existing releases and catalogue; no extra app, paid promotion or new collection form is required for this stage.
  2. Identify legitimate distribution channels and record release/channel attribution where measurement is available and privacy-appropriate.
  3. Observe reliable engagement and repeat-interest signals; separate qualitative feedback from anonymous view counts.
  4. Record concrete audience requests and collaboration enquiries privately and with appropriate consent.
  5. Pre-register simple decision thresholds when baseline data and a feasible sampling method exist; mark inconclusive results as inconclusive.

04 / Decision rules

Proceed, refine, or hold.

Proceed to a focused next test only when reliable repeat-interest evidence supports a specific hypothesis. Refine when response clusters around particular songs, formats or audiences. Hold when measurement is absent or insufficient; do not infer success or failure from vanity metrics.

No experiment cohort, data baseline, commercial offer or numeric success threshold has been approved or registered for XYS-001 yet.
