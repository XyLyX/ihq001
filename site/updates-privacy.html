Who is responsible

Your subscription request

We collect your email, optional role, affirmative consent and notice version to confirm your request and send IdeaHQ founder updates. Updates are intended monthly, with occasional meaningful announcements. We do not require a role to subscribe. This subscription is separate from any project research or commercial engagement.

How it works now

Netlify hosts the form and privately stores submissions. It processes request information for security and spam prevention and may use infrastructure or subprocessors outside the UAE, including the United States. See Netlify’s privacy policy. Navvin personally reviews requests and replies from navvin@ideahq.io to confirm your interest before adding you to the active list. No automated newsletter platform is currently connected. We will update this notice and inform subscribers before using a new mailing provider. The form does not verify ownership of an email address by itself.

Your choices and retention

You may decline, withdraw consent or request access, correction or deletion by emailing navvin@ideahq.io. Every founder update must include a clear unsubscribe option; while sending is manual, replying “unsubscribe” is sufficient. We will stop future updates and remove the active contact. A minimal restricted suppression record may be kept to prevent accidental re-addition. Unconfirmed requests will be deleted within 30 days. Active subscriptions are reviewed after 90 days of inactivity and removed if no longer needed. We do not sell contact details or publish subscriber identities, and we do not share the list with project operators.

No optional visitor analytics or advertising tracking is enabled here. Emailing a research project is not consent to this list. Questions or complaints can be raised with Navvin at the address above.
