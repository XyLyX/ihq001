Who is responsible

Navvin S Bhalla, Dubai, UAE, conducts this research personally under the IdeaHQ concept name. Contact: idhq1@ideahq.io. All project correspondence uses this address. No registered company or operating licence is represented by this notice.

What we collect and why

If you submit the research form, we collect your email and selected answers about residence, foreign-investor status, property scope, ownership stage, timing, value band, rental model, principal problem and interest in paid scope. If you request more detail, we also collect your short question to tailor our reply. Please do not include sensitive information in that note. We also record the concept variant, experiment, allowlisted outreach source and notice version. We use this information to assess research eligibility, verify your email by personal reply, arrange a conversation if appropriate, and assess demand. Participation is voluntary and based on your research consent. Optional future product emails require separate consent and are not a condition of participation.

Service providers and access

The site and form processing use Netlify. Netlify stores submissions, processes spam checks and handles request information such as IP addresses and browser details for hosting/security. Its infrastructure and subprocessors may process information outside the UAE, including the United States. Read Netlify’s privacy policy. Research replies use the IdeaHQ email aliases and the controller’s email service. Navvin personally reviews and replies to participants. Identifiable submissions and interview notes must not be placed in the public GitHub source repository or shared with prospective property operators.

Retention and choices

Research contact data and identifiable notes will be deleted within 30 days after the experiment closes, unless you separately agree to a next step. Only anonymous aggregate results will remain. If you separately opt into product updates, your email and consent evidence may be kept until you unsubscribe or a 90-day inactivity review. Ask to stop future contact, withdraw consent, access, correct or delete your data by emailing idhq1@ideahq.io. Withdrawal does not change processing that already occurred. We will review requests and any applicable legal exceptions and provide the relevant complaint route on request.

Analytics and limits

No optional visitor analytics, advertising cookies or fingerprinting are enabled in this initial version. We record the outreach source supplied with a form but do not track anonymous visits across pages. The form provider may reject spam; opening a thank-you page does not establish a verified lead or scheduled appointment. This is research into proposed services, not a property management, investment, tax or brokerage service. Do not submit passports, exact property addresses, bank information or financial documents.

Privacy questions or concerns: idhq1@ideahq.io.
