Proposed live research use

Before launch, identify the legal data controller, its address and a working privacy/contact email here. These details have not been supplied. Do not launch while they are missing.

Proposed collection: email and selected answers about residence, foreign-investor status, one ready property, ownership stage, timeline, budget band, rental preference, problem and willingness to discuss paid scope. No identity documents, bank details, exact address or property documents are requested.

Proposed purpose: determine eligibility for IHQ-001, reply about this research, and analyse demand. Research consent is required to participate; future product updates and non-essential analytics require separate optional consent. Declining optional consent must not prevent participation.

Proposed retention: delete identifiable research data within 30 days after the experiment closes, unless a separately agreed next step requires it. Retain only anonymous aggregate counts. If product updates are selected, retain minimal contact and consent evidence until withdrawal or a 90-day inactivity review. Final periods must be approved before launch.

Live notice must name processors, hosting and email providers, storage countries, transfer safeguards, recipients and the applicable rights process. Do not sell contact data or share it with brokers or operators without a separate disclosed basis and consent where required. Restrict access to named research staff.

Participants should be able to request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal through the published contact. Withdrawal should stop future research contact; optional updates must have an unsubscribe route. State any applicable exceptions and complaint route after jurisdiction review. International outreach may trigger additional local privacy rules.

These are proposed operating choices, not a statement of legal compliance. The controller and privacy reviewer must approve the final notice and implement it before collecting data.
