Homepage and /notes/ share the native Netlify form ideahq-founder-updates. Email, explicit unchecked consent and optional role are collected; hidden subject is [IdeaHQ | Founder Updates] Subscription request. No auto-newsletter sender, scheduler or LinkedIn posting integration is installed. User-supplied LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/navvyn. No external posts or emails sent.

Before any mailing: privately review consent and spam, manually request confirmation from navvin@ideahq.io, activate only on a positive reply, deduplicate email addresses and exclude QA. Never treat provider Verified submissions as confirmed ownership. Delete unconfirmed requests after 30 days; review active list after 90 days inactivity; honour unsubscribe replies and keep minimal suppression privately. Never commit contact lists or subscriber details to public GitHub. Every update needs an unsubscribe instruction. Monthly is the intended editorial cadence, not an active automation.

Notifications: scope each research notification to its own form and nyk@ideahq.io. Founder form notifications must go only to navvin@ideahq.io. Do not leave a project-inbox catch-all when adding this separate form.

Opening note is a dated founder introduction with no invented validation results. Search indexing remains disabled pending a separate decision; adding Notes does not itself distribute content or notify anyone.
