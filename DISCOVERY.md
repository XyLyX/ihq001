# Verified Stays — 15-minute discovery script
Draft outreach sender: Navvin — IdeaHQ <navvin@ideahq.io>. General research contact: hello@ideahq.io. Addresses are user-supplied, not delivery-verified. No messages or calendar invitations have been sent. No scheduling provider is connected; do not display a fake booking link.

## Opening / minute 0–1
“Thank you for helping with this research. IdeaHQ is testing a proposed service; we do not have a management service available through this test. This conversation is not investment or tax advice. You can skip questions or stop. May I take brief notes for this experiment?” Default to notes only. Do not record unless separately explained and explicitly agreed; specify access, retention and deletion before recording. Record consent or refusal.

## Segment / minute 1–3
Where do you live? Do you own, or plan to buy, exactly one completed Dubai unit? What rental arrangement do you use or consider? When will you next decide about management? Do not request a passport, exact address or bank information. Confirm eligibility separately from enthusiasm.

## Behaviour / minute 3–6
“Tell me about the last deduction or maintenance task you could not verify.” What did you do? How much time or money did it cost? What evidence did the manager provide? How often has this happened? What do you use now, and what do you already pay? Avoid assuming that a problem exists. Record no pain and satisfaction with current management as valid evidence.

## Trust trade-off / minute 6–9
Show the labelled mockup. “What would you need to see before accepting this expense?” Which matters most: invoice detail, named task owner, dated photos, approval rules or reporting? Could photos be misleading? How should emergency repairs and unavailable owner approvals work? Ask what would make them distrust the evidence. Never call the concept audit-certified or independently verified unless that process exists.

## Price and scope / minute 9–12
First ask their current fee and minimum acceptable scope, without anchoring. Then compare the same routine-management scope against the same scope plus evidence controls at an explicit hypothetical premium. Establish whether fees apply to rent collected, bookings or another base; state service markup, taxes, minimum fees and maintenance charges separately. Do not compare an undisclosed all-in fee with a percentage-only quote.

Research example ONLY, not a market benchmark or offer: “If basic management were 15% of rent collected, would you consider 18% for the same scope plus invoice evidence and approval controls?” Explain that three percentage points means AED 3 extra per AED 100 collected; neither rate is a real quote. Alternate basic-first / evidence-first presentation by interview number. Ask “Which, if either, and why?” Then ask the maximum premium they would consider and what would have to be proven. Never imply that regulated services or compliance guarantees are included.

## Action / minute 12–15
“If an appropriately authorised pilot with that scope and a written price became available, would you want to discuss it?” Ask who decides, timing, switching conditions and the next concrete step. Permission for one follow-up is separate from a purchase. Record explicit nonbinding paid discussion interest versus “sounds interesting”, cheaper-only preference and refusal. Thank them; offer withdrawal via the approved contact once finalised.

## Evidence coding
Track participant code, eligibility, source/variant, actual incident, current alternative, current fee if volunteered, urgent need, preferred proof, hypothetical price shown, order, basic/evidence/neither choice, acceptable premium, objection, attended status and separately agreed follow-up. Keep contact details outside public GitHub and aggregate reports. Do not paste identifiable interview notes into this repository.
