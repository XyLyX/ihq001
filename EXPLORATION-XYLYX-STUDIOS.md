# IdeaHQ exploration brief — XyLyX Studios (`XYS-001`)

**Experiment reference:** `XYS-001` (venture prefix XYS + sequential test number 001).

**Status:** Proposed first audience-validation experiment, not yet registered as an active measured cohort. Existing creative work is published; audience and commercial model are not validated.

**Source:** `https://github.com/XyLyX/xylyxstudios`, homepage, Works and Visuals source. Live website could not be fetched independently during this edit; source links and content were reviewed in GitHub.

## What exists
XyLyX Studios describes itself as a home for original songs, visual pieces and combinations of sound and image. The source website includes original releases *Tere Naa Di Kahaani* and *Peat by Pollution*, plus a *Wired Globe* visualizer.

## Hypothesis to stress-test
Can original songs, visual storytelling and audiovisual experiments generate a repeat audience that engages, returns and actively wants more work?

This is an **audience-validation** question. Publication of creative work is not validation of commercial demand.

## Minimum-cost discovery
1. Make the existing work easy to discover; do not build extra platforms, paid audience tools or features in advance.
2. Observe which works attract sustained interest, not just initial clicks or enthusiasm.
3. Test whether viewers return for subsequent releases and express specific interest in more music or visuals.
4. Collect permission-based qualitative feedback from listeners and potential collaborators.
5. Decide which experiment to try next from observed evidence, without presenting vanity metrics as business outcomes.

## Signals to examine when data is available
- Qualified interest in further releases (voluntary subscriptions, direct requests or repeat opt-in engagement).
- Repeat audience behaviour across distinct releases.
- Meaningful retention and completion indicators, if accurately measurable.
- Credible collaboration or commercial enquiries with concrete scope, not informal compliments.
- Cost and production time per release relative to observed engagement.

**No baseline measurements, minimum thresholds or monetisation claims are established by this page.** Agree those before interpreting results; do not back-fit benchmarks to observed metrics.
