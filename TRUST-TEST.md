# Supplementary trust-layer test · VS-001-T draft

Question: will overseas owners of one ready Dubai unit consider a management premium for traceable expenses and task evidence, beyond basic management? Route: /verified-stays. This is a follow-on cohort after the original comparison, not a fourth arm silently inserted into it. It may also run as a separate discovery exercise if dates, allocation and outcomes are separately registered. Preserve all original VS-001 counts.

No real-time dashboard, audit service, operator, licence or local team is claimed. “Verified Stays” is a concept name, not an independent certification. The portal illustration deliberately shows unverified status. A landing page cannot prove demand by itself. Scope clicks cannot establish premium willingness; a price-choice interview supplies additional nonbinding evidence, and paid pilot sales would need later validation.

Use the same target and shared form. Invite eligible respondents to a 15-minute research conversation, initially by verified email reply and manual time agreement. Do not use “portfolio review” to imply an advisory service or a multi-unit audience. Add a real scheduling link only after owner, availability, privacy and provider terms are confirmed. A booked call is secondary; an attended qualified call is stronger. Leave paid ads off.

Before traffic, register at least 100 measurable eligible visits, 8 qualified contacts and 5 attended interviews as the exploratory evidence floor, with matching cohort and follow-up rules from EXPERIMENT.md. A directional proceed-to-pilot-design signal requires >=3 of 5 or more attended, eligible interviewees choosing evidence at an explicit premium and explicitly requesting a later scoped paid-pilot discussion; >=2 must be outside close contacts. Record the premium and denominator, not just a yes. This is not statistical market validation or a payment commitment.

Iterate if owners report urgent mistrust but will not consider a premium or require proof the proposed operation cannot supply. Stop the premium proposition only with adequate exposure and interview evidence showing low urgency or consistent preference for basic pricing. Otherwise mark inconclusive. High CTR with low calls could reflect friction, trust, offer, targeting or scheduling; it does not uniquely identify bad pain framing. Low CTR plus low calls does not alone reject the segment.

Do not adopt the pasted USD 150 / eight calls rule or buy traffic: costs, permission and unit economics have not been approved. No claim is made about language demographics or ad-platform targeting availability. Test one language and permitted channel first; validate translated meaning and audience evidence before adding languages.
